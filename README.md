# Design references: hero compositions

Hero layouts from other industries, kept for composition ideas only. Borrow the technique, never the content: no logo strips, "trusted by" lines, countdowns, stat widgets, fake data panels or quotes.

| File | Technique to borrow |
|---|---|
| 01-floating-card-collage | Left text, right a tilted set of small cards (photo, mini quote card, chips, a tick) joined by thin dashed connector lines. The hero explains the service at a glance. One word in the headline has a hand-drawn underline. |
| 02-fullbleed-photo-with-looping-path | Full-bleed photo with a big white looping line winding through it and small round markers on it. Text overlaid, round buttons. |
| 03-arch-masked-photos-with-doodles | Real photos in arch and pill masks over brand-colour blocks, overlapping, with doodles (starburst, scribble, spark lines). Serif headline with one colour-highlighted word. |
| 04-framed-panel-curved-edge-subject-breaking-out | Large rounded image panel with a curved accent-colour edge along the bottom; the subject and thin orbit lines overlap the frame so it breaks out of it. |
| 05-cutout-subject-bleeding-off-edge | Clean cut-out subject bleeding off the right edge, serif headline, lots of calm space. (Needs a clean cut-out.) |
| 06-cutout-over-layered-shapes | Cut-out subject over layered shapes: a paint splash, a striped circle, dots, a diamond. |
| 07-oversized-letter-over-geometric-shapes | A giant letter as the hero graphic over simple geometric shapes and a dot grid. |

Common thread: the visual is composed from several layered pieces. It is never a single boxed photo with a drop shadow.
