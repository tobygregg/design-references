# Trade playbook

Starting ideas per trade. They are prompts for thinking, not templates: check `house-fingerprint.md` and pick the one the recent builds didn't use, and always prefer an idea that uses **this client's real photos and details**. Each trade lists several signature ideas so consecutive builds in the same trade still differ.

**Island-wide ideas (any trade):** an SVG parish map of Guernsey that lights up the parishes served (tap a parish → what we do there); a route line drawn across the island; the coastline as a section edge; granite texture used once; tide/water lines; a "St Peter Port → Vale" journey as the page's scroll path. Never a Google Map as the hero.

### Window, gutter & exterior cleaning
- **Signatures:** a drawn house whose windows clear as a pole passes (done: Pure Reflections — do it differently); a before/after *within one photo* revealed by a squeegee-shaped wipe on scroll; a "tap the house" explorer (done twice — rotate to a scroll-driven cross-section or a street of houses); a rota/calendar ring for 4- and 8-weekly rounds.
- **Hero options:** 12 (macro of a squeegee pass), 03 with window-arch masks, 07 (a Guernsey terrace street scene), 11 (the kit on brand colour).
- **Motif:** shine glints, a wipe arc, droplets, reflections. **Type feel:** clean, energetic (unbounded, tilt-warp, onest) or crisp grotesk.

### Plumbing, heating & renewables
- **Signatures:** flow-and-return pipes linking sections (done: Datum); a boiler/heat-pump cutaway with hotspots; a temperature dial the visitor turns to switch between services; a house cross-section showing the heating system; the van's toolkit as an explorer.
- **Hero options:** 04 (framed kit photo + label breaking out), 06 (giant "24/7"-style numeral only if they really offer it), 08, 14.
- **Motif:** pipe elbows, steam, a gauge needle. **Type feel:** industrial or technical (big-shoulders, geologica + martian-mono for numbers).

### Electricians, solar & EV
- **Signatures:** a cable run that "powers up" each section as it scrolls into view; a consumer unit whose switches are the services; a house whose rooms light up; a day/night solar arc.
- **Hero options:** 09 (services as giant type with a lit-up switch), 05 (van cut-out), 11.
- **Motif:** cable runs, switch toggles, spark ticks (small, never confetti). **Type feel:** recursive, chivo + chivo-mono, anybody.

### Gardening, landscaping & tree surgery
- **Signatures:** seasonal switcher (spring/summer/autumn/winter jobs); a garden plan drawn top-down with hotspots; growth rings or a felling-cut diagram for tree work; before/after of real gardens.
- **Hero options:** 02 (a hedge line or path drawn over a big garden photo), 14, 12, 03 with leaf-shaped masks.
- **Motif:** hedge-line edges, drifting leaves (pause offscreen), mower stripes. **Type feel:** heritage slab (bitter, rokkitt) or warm sans; avoid Fraunces/Bricolage (overused).

### Painting & decorating
- **Signatures:** a room that repaints itself as you scroll; paint-chip cards as the services explorer; a masking-tape reveal over before/after photos; a roller-wipe transition.
- **Hero options:** 11 (paint tins/brushes on the brand colour), 09, 04.
- **Motif:** brush stroke, masking tape, paint chips. **Type feel:** editorial (bodoni-moda, ibarra-real-nova) for high-end; signwriter slab for trade decorators.

### Roofing, building, plastering, pointing
- **Signatures:** a roof built up in layers (battens, membrane, slates) on scroll; a wall cross-section; a spirit-level bubble that settles on load; a project timeline drawn as a path.
- **Hero options:** 03 with roof-pitch cuts (Klindworth), 06 (wordmark edge to edge, Haven), 02.
- **Motif:** slate overlap, chevrons, blueprint grid (subtle), trowel swipe. **Type feel:** big-shoulders, stencil faces, brygada/besley for heritage builders.

### Taxis, couriers, removals
- **Signatures:** an island route the visitor scrubs (airport → harbour → town); the vehicle drawn as an interactive diagram (done: Premier Taxis — rotate); a booking flow told as a ticket/stub; journey times on a mini map.
- **Hero options:** 02 (route line over a real island photo), 05 (vehicle cut-out over a drawn street), 10 (name marquee).
- **Motif:** dashed route with stops, headlight sweep, meter digits. **Type feel:** van livery (anybody wide caps), stencil for removals.

### Bakers, cake makers, cafés
- **Signatures:** build-a-cake layers; a flat-lay hero (Bernice); a menu board; piping-line dividers; a "today's bakes" shelf.
- **Hero options:** 02 (flat-lay + giant type + sticker), 09 (bake names as giant type over a video window, Impasto), 11 (one bake cut out on colour), 13.
- **Motif:** sprinkles, piping, rolling pin. **Type feel:** chunky friendly serif (caprasimo, corben, bagel-fat-one), shrikhand already used once.

### Pet services (grooming, walking, sitting)
- **Signatures:** a walk route around a local spot; groom before/after; a lead line that connects sections; the dog's "day" as a timeline.
- **Hero options:** 10 (owner + dog portrait, name marquee), 11 (Wild One-style split with cut-outs), 13.
- **Motif:** one paw trail done well, a lead line, a wagging loop. **Type feel:** shantell-sans with bounce, grandstander, sour-gummy.

### Cleaning (domestic, end-of-tenancy, junk removal)
- **Signatures:** a clean stripe wiping through a grimy photo; a checklist that ticks itself room by room; a van filling up (junk removal) then empty.
- **Hero options:** 12, 09, 13 for scrappy friendly brands.
- **Motif:** bubbles (few), wipe stripes, ticks. **Type feel:** onest/golos, tilt-warp, rokkitt (used once).

### Locksmiths, security, mobile mechanics
- **Signatures:** a lock cylinder cutaway with pins; a key-cut profile as the divider; a car diagram with tappable parts (mechanic); a response-time ring (only if stated).
- **Hero options:** 06 (giant key-profile numeral/mark), 04, 08.
- **Motif:** key profiles, tyre treads, spanner arcs. **Type feel:** precise technical faces; stencil for security.
