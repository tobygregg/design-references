# Swap AI defaults for client-derived decisions

A website reads as AI "slop" when it is built from a recognisable cluster of statistical defaults. The cluster includes a centred hero with a pill badge and gradient headline, purple or indigo accents, Inter or another "AI font" (Space Grotesk, Geist, Fraunces, Instrument Serif), three identical icon-on-top cards, one radius and one soft shadow on everything, glass panels, stat strips, all-caps eyebrow labels, fade-up on every section, and copy that could belong to any company. The cluster is now measurable. In a deterministic scan of **1,590 Show HN sites, 22% showed four or more of these patterns** ([Krebs](https://adriankrebs.ch/blog/design-slop/)). Anthropic attributes the convergence to models sampling from the "high-probability center" of web training data ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/blog/improving-frontend-design-through-skills)). Distinctive work comes from making a small number of real decisions and holding them consistently. Each decision should be derived from the specific client: a palette sampled from the van livery, a characterful display face tuned through variable axes, a layout shaped by the content, real job photos with one consistent treatment, one custom trade motif, one orchestrated motion moment, and copy that names parishes, services and the owner. For a small Guernsey studio's automated routine (static HTML, Tailwind v3, vanilla JS, vendored assets), the practical recipe has five steps. Extract brand inputs at intake. Write a per-client design spec. Generate under an explicit ban list covering both the first wave of slop (purple, Inter) and the second wave (cream-and-terracotta, black-and-acid-green). Lint the rendered page against deterministic thresholds. Finish with a human review on a phone. Prompting alone moves the cluster rather than escaping it, so the routine needs both a direction and a filter.

## Slop is a measurable cluster of defaults, and banning it creates a second wave

The most useful finding in this research is that "AI look" is a set of specific, detectable choices, not a vague impression. Adrian Krebs ran a Playwright script that reads computed styles and DOM on Show HN submissions, with no LLM judging and an estimated **5–10% false-positive rate**. The high tier (4+ patterns) was 22%, medium (2–3) 32% and low (0–1) 46% ([Krebs](https://adriankrebs.ch/blog/design-slop/)). Per-pattern rates from his initial 500-site sample show which defaults dominate: extensive gradients on **28%**, default-state shadcn/ui on 23.5%, centred title on 23.5%, always-dark mode with grey text and uppercase labels on 20.3%, templated 3- or 6-card feature grids on 20.1%, glass panels on 17.1%, AI-favoured fonts on 15.8%, a coloured accent stripe on card edges on 13%, stat banners on 12.2%, and purple CTA buttons on 10.7% ([GIGAZINE summary](https://www.gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260423-design-slop)). One caveat matters for this studio: the sample is developer products, and **no equivalent measurement exists for local trade sites**.

The cause is structural. Anthropic calls it **"distributional convergence"**: "Safe design choices, those that work universally and offend no one, dominate web training data". Unguided output therefore lands on an average that is "immediately recognizable—and dismissible" ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/blog/improving-frontend-design-through-skills)). Its own cookbook concedes that Claude "defaults to simplistic designs with white and purple backgrounds" without guidance ([Anthropic Cookbook](https://platform.claude.com/cookbook/coding-prompting-for-frontend-aesthetics)). The purple itself has a traceable origin. Tailwind UI used `bg-indigo-500` as a neutral demo colour, and in August 2025 Adam Wathan apologised for making every button indigo ([prg.sh](https://prg.sh/ramblings/Why-Your-AI-Keeps-Building-the-Same-Purple-Gradient-Website)). That second-hand account is not universally accepted: CodeMySpec argues the purple comes from "a model filling a blank, not from shadcn, which ships near-black on white" ([CodeMySpec](https://codemyspec.com/blog/vibe-coded-websites-look-the-same)). Either way, a Tailwind-based routine is exposed. Unless colours, radius, shadow and font are overridden at the `tailwind.config.js` level, the framework's defaults leak into every component.

The finding with the largest consequences for an automated builder is the **second wave**. Once purple and Inter are banned, models drift to three new defaults. The first is "warm editorial": cream around `#F4F1EA`, a high-contrast serif and terracotta around `#D97757`, which is Anthropic's own Claude accent. The second is "dark signal": near-black with one acid-green or vermilion accent. The third is "broadsheet cosplay": hairline rules, zero radius and newspaper columns on non-editorial content ([Capital & Compute](https://capitalandcompute.net/blog/fix-ai-slop-design/)). The same thing has happened to fonts. Anthropic's October 2025 cookbook recommended Fraunces and Space Grotesk as escapes from Inter, and by 2026 both are on slop catalogues ([Impeccable](https://git.secluded.site/agent-skills/blob/main/vendor/impeccable/site/pages/slop/index.astro)). Researchers studying vibe-coded sites conclude that homogenisation is "not solvable through prompt engineering alone". They propose "productive friction": clarifying questions about brand and context before generation ([arXiv 2603.13036 summary](https://ai-paper-delta.vercel.app/en/papers/2603.13036)). Practitioners reach the same conclusion: "A filter removes defaults but cannot supply a look. A direction... supplies the look but does not remove defaults. You need both" ([Capital & Compute](https://capitalandcompute.net/blog/fix-ai-slop-design/)).

The table below condenses the cross-source catalogue into tells and fixes for each dimension in the question.

| Dimension | Slop tell | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Centred H1, pill badge above, gradient text, two buttons, logo row ([Pixelslop](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/pixelslop@0.3.8/dist/skill/resources/ai-slop-patterns.md)) | Lead with "the most characteristic thing in the product's world" ([C&C](https://capitalandcompute.net/blog/fix-ai-slop-design/)) |
| Layout | 3 identical icon-title-blurb cards; same section shape 3+ times; >70% centred text ([Pixelslop](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/pixelslop@0.3.8/dist/skill/resources/ai-slop-patterns.md)) | Asymmetry, varied section compositions, lists or tables for list content, inline icons ([Impeccable](https://git.secluded.site/agent-skills/blob/main/vendor/impeccable/site/pages/slop/index.astro)) |

| Colour | Purple/indigo gradients; pure #000/#fff; glowing dark mode; grey text on colour ([Impeccable](https://git.secluded.site/agent-skills/blob/main/vendor/impeccable/site/pages/slop/index.astro)) | 4–6 named colours with roles, tinted neutrals, no gradient text ([C&C](https://capitalandcompute.net/blog/fix-ai-slop-design/)) |

| Motion | Same fade-up on every section, bounce easing, count-up stats, `transition-all` ([Pixelslop](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/pixelslop@0.3.8/dist/skill/resources/ai-slop-patterns.md)) | "One orchestrated moment"; animate in response to user action ([C&C](https://capitalandcompute.net/blog/fix-ai-slop-design/)) |

The stakes are commercial. Visitors form a credibility judgement in **about 50 ms**, driven by visual appeal ([Utsubo, citing Lindgaard et al.](https://www.utsubo.com/blog/built-with-ai-trust-signals-2026)).

## Heroes and page rhythm should lead with the job, the phone number and the parish

For a trade site, the AI-default hero is easy to predict: "Professional Window Cleaning Services You Can Trust" centred over a gradient, a pill badge, "Reliable. Affordable. Professional." and a "Get Started" button. Below it come three icon cards for "Our Services", a "500+ happy customers" stat strip, a 1-2-3 "How it works" row and uniform `rounded-xl shadow-md` cards. The numbered step sequence alone appears on **9.4%** of sampled sites ([GIGAZINE](https://www.gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260423-design-slop)). The remedy is to choose the hero archetype from the client's strongest real asset. Anthropic's own skill tells the model to "commit to a bold aesthetic direction" and define "the one thing people will remember". It also allows restraint: "Elegance comes from executing the vision well, not from adding more" ([Anthropic frontend-design skill](https://www.mintlify.com/anthropics/skills/reference/frontend-design)).

Verified small-business evidence is thin but instructive. **Lune Croissanterie** uses a full-width looping video of a single croissant with play/skip controls, a one-sentence craft claim ("Lune is an institution solely dedicated to the creation of croissants.") and a prominent ORDER NOW CTA ([Lune](https://www.lunecroissanterie.com/)). The trade equivalent is one obsessive macro-shot, such as a squeegee pass revealing clear glass, plus one blunt sentence. **Bernice Bakery** in Montreal won an Awwwards Honorable Mention in February 2025 for a "fun, dynamic and playful" custom Shopify build with "many unique animations" that still lets customers shop ([Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/bernice-bakery)). **Klindworth Roofing** took an Honorable Mention in April 2024 with a tan/bronze `#ac9376` on black palette ([Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/klindworth-roofing)), which proves trade sites can win craft awards. Its palette also sits close to the dark-premium look the routine should treat with suspicion. Mainstream "hero inspiration" lists skew heavily to SaaS: Marketer Milk's 30 examples are almost all tech products ([Marketer Milk](https://marketermilk.com/blog/hero-section-examples)). Those lists, and the generic "best plumber websites" round-ups, are better used as anti-examples than as references.

The research catalogued about 18 hero archetypes. Several well-known brands are cited as examples, but they could not be verified live in this session and should be checked before use as references: Octopus Energy's illustrated mascot for a "boring" utility ([octopus.energy](https://octopus.energy/)), product-on-colour-field brands such as Graza and Lick paint ([graza.co](https://www.graza.co/), [lick.com](https://www.lick.com/)), and sticker or neo-brutalist work such as Milk Bar and Gumroad ([milkbarstore.com](https://milkbarstore.com/), [gumroad.com](https://gumroad.com/)). The research's judgement is that the archetypes realistic for a one-person studio need only good photography, CSS and a little SVG. The best candidates for trade clients are below.

| Archetype | Trade translation | Breaks when | Mobile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full-bleed photo/video + one claim | Real footage of the work, a blunt craft sentence | Stock video; text over a busy image without a scrim | Portrait poster crop; CTA above the fold |
| Oversized type / numeral | Giant phone number or initial; services marquee ("GUTTERS • WINDOWS • CONSERVATORIES") | Weak logo blown up; illegible crop | Excellent; scales with viewport units |
| Product-on-colour-field | The actual kit (squeegee, copper fitting, paint tin) on a brand-colour backdrop | Poor phone photos; colour fights the logo | Very robust |
| Cut-out subject + marquee | Owner with ladder or spanner on a brand field, business name scrolling behind | Halo-edged cut-outs; marquee too fast | Works well |
| Hand-drawn annotation over photo | "this is Darren", "yes, we do conservatories" | Clip-art doodle packs | SVG scales; reposition the notes |
| Sticker/scrapbook | Polaroid job photos, a "5★ on Google" sticker (cakes, groomers) | Stickers cover the CTA | Fewer stickers, flow layout |
| Illustrated SVG parish map | Coverage across the island as a selling point | Embedded Google Map as the hero | SVG scales cleanly |
| Before/after or hotspot photo | Tappable gutters, fascias, windows with prices | Hover-only, tiny targets | ≥44 px targets, list fallback |

The research flags arch/pill-masked photos with doodles, floating card collages with connector lines, and curved-edge panels with a subject breaking out as patterns found mainly in Dribbble shots and Framer/Webflow templates, with no celebrated live examples. They carry high template risk unless the mask echoes a real brand shape, such as a window arch for a window cleaner. 3D/WebGL and scroll-telling are the most expensive archetypes and the least suited to trades.

Below the hero, rhythm matters as much as the hero itself. "Uniform everything" means "nothing leads the eye". The fix is to make the primary element "bigger, closer, and heavier" ([Kosta Canatselis](https://world.hey.com/kostac/spot-the-slop-a-ui-designer-s-guide-to-fixing-ai-defaults-4c448c9c)). Group content with tight internal spacing and larger section gaps. Use one primary button per view, with ghost or text buttons for secondary actions. Centre only heroes and CTAs ([Impeccable](https://git.secluded.site/agent-skills/blob/main/vendor/impeccable/site/pages/slop/index.astro)). Pixelslop flags three or more consecutive sections with the same internal structure ([Pixelslop](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/pixelslop@0.3.8/dist/skill/resources/ai-slop-patterns.md)). A good trade page therefore alternates its compositions: a full-bleed job-photo band, a left-aligned text-and-photo split, the services as a dense list or price table rather than cards, one large named review, a gallery strip of real jobs, and a contact block with the parish map. A UK trades-web agency adds non-negotiables that double as authenticity signals. Show real photos of completed work and genuine reviews from named customers. Display accreditations clearly. State the service area in body copy, not just the footer. Keep the **phone number visible without scrolling on every device**, and don't make contact form-only ([b:web](https://bwebsites.co.uk/news/what-makes-a-good-website-for-a-trades-business/)).

## Typography carries the brand when one display face is chosen and its variable axes are pushed

Changing the font is the fastest single fix. One designer puts it at about an hour ([Kosta Canatselis](https://world.hey.com/kostac/spot-the-slop-a-ui-designer-s-guide-to-fixing-ai-defaults-4c448c9c)). The banned list, however, has grown. Impeccable lists **Inter, Roboto, Fraunces, Geist, Plus Jakarta Sans and Space Grotesk** as overused ([Impeccable](https://git.secluded.site/agent-skills/blob/main/vendor/impeccable/site/pages/slop/index.astro)). Krebs's detector adds Instrument Serif and Syne ([GIGAZINE](https://www.gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260423-design-slop)). Anthropic admits Claude still converges on "Space Grotesk, for example" across generations ([Anthropic Cookbook](https://platform.claude.com/cookbook/coding-prompting-for-frontend-aesthetics)). A pattern emerges: **any font named in a widely used prompt becomes the next tell**. The same cookbook's recommended lists include Playfair Display, Crimson Pro, Bricolage Grotesque, Newsreader and IBM Plex ([Anthropic Cookbook](https://platform.claude.com/cookbook/coding-prompting-for-frontend-aesthetics)). The routine should treat these as amber rather than safe and should never let the model pick a font unprompted. The studio's existing exclusion list (Outfit, Gloock, Archivo Black, Barlow Condensed, Geist, Playfair) should be merged with the detector lists into one hard ban.

The Fontsource pool outside those lists is deep. Every package below was confirmed on npm as OFL-1.1 (a few Apache-2.0) at version 5.3.x, which makes them safe to self-host. Variable packages register a family name with a " Variable" suffix (e.g. `'Bricolage Grotesque Variable'`), and that exact name must appear in Tailwind's `fontFamily` config. Specific axes are loaded through per-axis CSS files such as `opsz.css`, `wdth.css` or `full.css` ([npm @fontsource-variable/fraunces](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource-variable/fraunces)). Fontshare favourites (Satoshi, Clash Display, General Sans, Cabinet Grotesk) and most Velvetyne fonts are **not on Fontsource**. Bagnard is the exception. Variable axes are the strongest distinctiveness lever, because one family can be tuned per client. Anybody spans **wdth 50–150**. Shantell Sans exposes "bounce" (BNCE −100–100) and "informality" (INFM 0–100). Recursive has casual (CASL) and mono (MONO) axes. Roboto Serif and Google Sans Flex have optical size, width and grade, and Google Sans Flex adds ROND ([npm @fontsource-variable/anybody](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource-variable/anybody), [shantell-sans](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource-variable/shantell-sans), [recursive](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource-variable/recursive), [google-sans-flex](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource-variable/google-sans-flex)).

The pairings below are design judgements built on that verified availability. They deliberately avoid the detector lists.

| Trade feel | Display | Body / utility | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Industrial signage (roofing, scaffolding, plumbing vans) | `big-shoulders-display` 800–900 caps | `ibm-plex-sans` + `ibm-plex-mono` for prices and phone numbers | Plex is amber (cookbook-listed); swap for `hanken-grotesk` + `martian-mono` to rotate |
| Vehicle livery (taxis, electricians) | `anybody` at wdth 120–150, wght 800 | `hanken-grotesk` or `schibsted-grotesk` | Expanded caps read like van graphics |
| Friendly local shop (bakers, cafés) | `young-serif` or `caprasimo` | `albert-sans` or `rethink-sans` | Chunky serif warmth without cream-and-terracotta |
| Signwriter heritage (joiners, family firms) | `alfa-slab-one` or `bevan` | `bitter` (variable) | Wood-type feel |
| Hand-made but legible (groomers, pet sitters) | `shantell-sans` at INFM 50–100, BNCE 20–50 | Same family at INFM 0, or `atkinson-hyperlegible-next` | One family, two personalities |
| Tech-leaning (solar, smart-home electricians) | `recursive` CASL 1 | `recursive` CASL 0 / MONO 1 for data | Single-family system |
| Energetic newcomer (valeting, cleaning) | `unbounded` | `onest` or `golos-text` | Rounded, wide |
| Heavy grotesk rotation | `bowlby-one`, `dela-gothic-one`, `boldonse`, `league-gothic` | `figtree`, `gabarito`, `parkinsans` | Replaces Archivo Black / Outfit |
| Trustworthy "local paper" | `brygada-1918`, `besley`, `petrona` or `imbue` | `public-sans` or `red-hat-text` | Avoids the cookbook-listed Newsreader/Literata |
| Annotation accent only | `caveat` or `kalam` | — | Captions and notes; never body text |

Scale and spacing rules come from two sources that look contradictory but aren't. Anthropic pushes display contrast hard: "Use extremes: 100/200 weight vs 800/900, not 400 vs 600. Size jumps of 3x+, not 1.5x" ([Anthropic Cookbook](https://platform.claude.com/cookbook/coding-prompting-for-frontend-aesthetics)). Impeccable recommends fewer sizes at about a 1.25 ratio ([Impeccable](https://git.secluded.site/agent-skills/blob/main/vendor/impeccable/site/pages/slop/index.astro)). The resolution is a dramatic jump between the hero display and everything else, with a modest ratio within body hierarchy. Butterick's practical rules set the floor: body text 15–25 px on the web, line length 45–90 characters, line spacing 120–145%, and **5–12% extra letterspacing on all caps**. He also says to use centred text sparingly and never to combine bold with italic ([Practical Typography](https://practicaltypography.com/summary-of-key-rules.html)). Impeccable adds a 65–75ch measure and no justified text, and reserves monospace for code and tabular data rather than using it as "technical" decoration ([Impeccable](https://git.secluded.site/agent-skills/blob/main/vendor/impeccable/site/pages/slop/index.astro)). In practice, the routine should set body at 17–19 px with leading of about 1.55, tighten display leading to 0.95–1.1 with −0.01 to −0.03em tracking, and push optical size with `font-variation-settings: "opsz" 96` (or the axis maximum) on heroes. It should load only the latin and latin-ext subsets and at most two families plus one accent.

## Colour should start from the van, and every hue needs a job

Anthropic's guidance on colour is direct: "Dominant colors with sharp accents outperform timid, evenly-distributed palettes" ([Anthropic Cookbook](https://platform.claude.com/cookbook/coding-prompting-for-frontend-aesthetics)). Practitioners add that each colour should be tied to "something real in your business" ([CodeMySpec](https://codemyspec.com/blog/vibe-coded-websites-look-the-same)). Utsubo's version is to build around the actual brand "even if the hue is slightly unusual" ([Utsubo](https://www.utsubo.com/blog/built-with-ai-trust-signals-2026)). For trade clients the true source already exists in the van wrap, the flyer, the shopfront sign or the logo. Sampling from it escapes both the purple wave and the cream/terracotta wave, because the model never chooses the hue.

The derivation should happen in OKLCH. HSL lightness varies across hues, whereas OKLCH's L is perceptually consistent. In OKLCH, "All backgrounds with L≥87% have good contrast with black text". Relative colour syntax such as `oklch(from var(--accent) calc(l + 0.1) c h)` derives hover and pressed states from one source token. Chroma must stay below about 0.37, and out-of-gamut values clip and can shift hue ([Evil Martians](https://evilmartians.com/chronicles/oklch-in-css-why-quit-rgb-hsl)). The recipe is to keep the brand hue as the dominant colour (Tailwind 600, used for primary buttons, the header band and key headings) and step L from about 0.97 to 0.25 for 50–950, tapering chroma toward both ends. Add a darker or neighbouring-hue support colour for footer and section grounds. Reserve one high-chroma complementary accent for CTAs, highlights and annotation marks at roughly 10% of the surface. The neutrals should be **the brand hue at chroma 0.005–0.02**, not Tailwind's slate or gray. Refactoring UI says you need 8–10 greys, one or two primaries with 5–10 shades, and sparing state accents. It advises fixing shades up front rather than relying on lighten()/darken(), and avoiding pure black because "true black tends to look pretty unnatural" ([Refactoring UI](https://www.refactoringui.com/previews/building-your-color-palette)). CodeMySpec gives a worked example of tinted neutrals: ground `#fdfcf9`, ink `#23211b`, and three meaningful brand colours ([CodeMySpec](https://codemyspec.com/blog/vibe-coded-websites-look-the-same)). Radix's 12-step scale is a ready-made role map, and its MIT package can be vendored. Steps 1–2 are backgrounds, 3–5 component states, 6–8 borders, 9–10 solids and 11–12 text. **Steps 11 and 12 are guaranteed to reach APCA Lc 60 and Lc 90** on step 2 ([Radix Colors](https://www.radix-ui.com/colors/docs/palette-composition/understanding-the-scale)).

Contrast needs two checks. WCAG 2 AA 4.5:1 is the compliance floor that Impeccable also cites ([Impeccable](https://git.secluded.site/agent-skills/blob/main/vendor/impeccable/site/pages/slop/index.astro)). APCA is the better perceptual test: **Lc 90 preferred and Lc 75 minimum for body text**, Lc 60 for medium text and Lc 45 for large headings. APCA is still only a WCAG 3 candidate, not normative ([Myndex APCA](https://github.com/Myndex/apca-introduction)). For build-time scripting, note the licences: `culori` and `colorjs.io` are MIT and Adobe's `leonardo-contrast-colors` is Apache-2.0, but `apca-w3` carries a "Limited W3 License" ([npm apca-w3](https://www.npmjs.com/package/apca-w3)). The common failure in trade palettes is white text on mid-saturation brand blues, greens and oranges. Use the 700–800 shade for text-bearing buttons, and put dark text on yellows and ambers. When a gradient is genuinely wanted, make it a single-hue tonal ramp interpolated with `linear-gradient(in oklch, …)`, or a grain-textured solid. Never use indigo-to-violet, and never use gradient text.

The starting points below are design judgements, not sourced associations. Each must be adjusted to the client's real livery.

| Trade | Dominant | Accent | Neutral ground |
|---|---|---|---|
| Window cleaning | Clear sky or sea blue | Squeegee yellow | Cool tinted white |
| Plumbing and heating | Deep navy or teal | Copper/brass orange | Blue-tinted grey |
| Gardening | Moss or bottle green | Terracotta (as an accent only, not on cream) | Warm stone |
| Painting and decorating | Charcoal or near-black | One paint-chip hue (ochre, sage, gold) | Warm off-white |
| Roofing | Slate grey-blue | Safety orange or brick red | Granite grey |
| Taxis and electricians | Black or charcoal | Cab yellow or high-voltage yellow, behind dark text | Tinted off-white |
| Guernsey-local motifs | Channel-sea teal, granite grey | Gorse yellow | Guernsey-cow fawn and cream |

For dark-premium clients such as black-and-gold brands, the research supports a deliberate dark theme with tinted darks, restrained elevation and one brand-derived accent checked for contrast. It rules out glow and neon, and it rules out defaulting to dark because it feels premium ([Pixelslop](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/pixelslop@0.3.8/dist/skill/resources/ai-slop-patterns.md)). The colour tools have distinct jobs. Coolors offers image extraction and Tailwind export, and Huemint previews palettes on page templates ([Muzli](https://muz.li/blog/best-color-palette-tools-and-generators-for-designers-2026/)). uicolors.app generates a Tailwind 50–950 scale from one hex ([uicolors.app](https://uicolors.app/generate/ff8a80-33eeff-3388ff)), and oklch.com and Huetone handle gamut checks and OKLCH/APCA scale building ([Evil Martians](https://evilmartians.com/chronicles/oklch-in-css-why-quit-rgb-hsl)).

## One custom motif and consistently treated real photos beat any illustration library

Branding for a trade client should fit on a single page per client, held as `brand.md` plus the Tailwind theme. It needs the mark (primary, compact and mono versions), colour roles with scales, the fixed type pair and axis values, one motif, one photo treatment, illustration rules (stroke width, corner radius, fill style) and three voice adjectives. The logo usually needs rescuing. Ask the signwriter or van-wrap printer for the original vector first. Failing that, vectorise. **VTracer (MIT)** handles colour images as well as binary ones. It offers spline mode, stacked or cutout hierarchy, speckle filtering of 0–128 px and colour precision of 1–8 bits, and installs via cargo, pip or npm ([VTracer](https://github.com/visioncortex/vtracer)). The Node port of Potrace is **GPL-2.0**, which is fine as a build-time tool but must not ship in client bundles ([npm potrace](https://www.npmjs.com/package/potrace)). A reasonable starting configuration for colour logos is `--mode spline --hierarchical stacked --filter-speckle 6 --color-precision 6`. After tracing, snap colours to the palette, run SVGO, and replace traced lettering with real type where the font is identifiable.

The motif is where hand-made character concentrates. Generic thin-line icons "could illustrate almost any product" ([925Studios](https://www.925studios.co/blog/ai-slop-design-tells)), and the icon-tile-above-heading card is "the common AI feature-card template" ([Impeccable](https://git.secluded.site/agent-skills/blob/main/vendor/impeccable/site/pages/slop/index.astro)). The replacement is one trade-specific graphic idea, drawn once as inline SVG with `currentColor` and reused as dividers, bullets, background pattern and 404 art. Examples: a squeegee-arc wipe for a window cleaner, pipe runs with elbows connecting process steps for a plumber, brush-stroke underlines and paint-chip cards for a decorator, a hedge-line edge for a gardener, a slate-overlap pattern for a roofer, and the Guernsey coastline or granite sea-wall texture for island identity. Icons are still needed, but only inline and only where they carry meaning. Every major set is permissively licensed (Phosphor MIT, Tabler MIT, Lucide ISC, Iconoir MIT, Remix Apache-2.0). Hand-drawn-style Iconify sets such as Streamline Freehand and Pepicons Pencil are **CC BY 4.0 and require visible credit** ([npm @iconify-json/streamline-freehand](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@iconify-json/streamline-freehand), [npm @phosphor-icons/core](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@phosphor-icons/core)). Phosphor's six weights, including duotone tinted with the accent, give the routine variation between clients. Fix the stroke width to match the body weight.

For a cheap human layer, **Rough Notation** (MIT, about 3.8 KB gz) draws animated hand-drawn underlines, circles, boxes, highlights and brackets, and can sequence them with `annotationGroup` ([Rough Notation](https://github.com/rough-stuff/rough-notation)). Use it on one or two words per page, such as the price, "same-day" or "free quote", and set `animate: false` under reduced motion. Atmosphere should come from texture, not glass. Anthropic recommends noise textures, geometric patterns, layered transparencies and grain overlays instead of flat defaults ([Anthropic frontend-design skill](https://www.mintlify.com/anthropics/skills/reference/frontend-design)). The standard CSS grain technique is an SVG `feTurbulence` fractal-noise rect (baseFrequency 0.65, 3 octaves) under the fill, sharpened with `contrast(170%) brightness(1000%)`. It is optionally blended with `mix-blend-mode: multiply` inside an `isolation: isolate` group, and blend modes render slightly differently in Blink and WebKit ([CSS-Tricks](https://css-tricks.com/grainy-gradients/)). Hero Patterns is recolourable but CC BY 4.0 ([Hero Patterns](https://heropatterns.com/)). Illustration libraries are mostly a trap for trades. Muzli notes that most free illustration sites offer variations of "the same person pointing at a screen" ([Muzli](https://muz.li/blog/best-free-illustration-libraries-for-designers-2026/)). Licences need care too. unDraw is often described as MIT, but its own licence forbids redistribution "in packs or otherwise" and use for AI training ([unDraw licence](https://undraw.co/license)), which matters for an automated pipeline that vendors assets. Open Peeps (CC0) is the closest to hand-made among libraries, but real photos plus a custom motif beat all of them.

Imagery is the most powerful authenticity signal and the one AI cannot fake. Utsubo flags "plastic" AI illustrations, 3D blobs and generated photos with "garbled signage or malformed hands", and recommends real photography, real team photos and named customers ([Utsubo](https://www.utsubo.com/blog/built-with-ai-trust-signals-2026)). Pixelslop flags stock-photo alt text such as "diverse team" and a uniform hover-zoom on every image ([Pixelslop](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/pixelslop@0.3.8/dist/skill/resources/ai-slop-patterns.md)). Client phone photos vary in quality, so consistency matters more than polish. Pick one treatment per site. The cheapest is a brand-900 overlay at 20–40% with `mix-blend-mode: multiply`, which also secures hero text contrast. Next is a light global grade. The richest is a true gradient-map duotone in SVG: desaturate with `feColorMatrix`, then map shadows and highlights to two brand colours with `feComponentTransfer` `tableValues`, each channel value divided by 255, under `color-interpolation-filters="sRGB"` ([Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2019/02/05/svg-filter-effects-duotone-images-with-fecomponenttransfer/)). Never duotone before-and-after photos, because true colour is the proof of the work. Fix aspect ratios site-wide, set `object-position` per image, pair before/after shots at identical crops, and caption them specifically ("Fascia & gutter clean, St Martin, March 2026"). Strip EXIF location data from customer photos and get permission before publishing.

## Motion earns its place by responding to input, and the vendored stack stays under 70 KB

Crafted motion is short, varied and purposeful. NN/g puts simple feedback at about 100 ms and most movement at 100–400 ms, with entrances slightly longer than exits. It warns that "It is far more common for animations to be too long than too short" and that at 500 ms animations "start to feel like a real drag". It also says to avoid linear easing, prefer ease-out for entrances, and treat parallax, carousel auto-advance and scroll-jacking as vestibular hazards ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/animation-duration/)). Emil Kowalski's rules complement this: usually under 300 ms, no animation on keyboard-initiated or frequent actions, transform and opacity only, CSS transitions for interruptible states, and a simple fade in place of movement under reduced motion ([Emil Kowalski](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/great-animations)). Codrops' breakdown of Eduard Bodak's portfolio shows how varied easing reads as crafted. It uses `elastic.out(1,0.75)` for settles, `sine.inOut` for idle floats with a **randomised duration per element** (`gsap.utils.random(1.5, 2.5)`), `power2.out` for pointer-follow and linear only for 1:1 scroll tracking. It adds a tiny `index * 0.012` stagger to avoid lockstep motion, gates pointer effects to `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`, and gives mobile a simpler variant ([Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/07/29/built-to-move-a-closer-look-at-the-animations-behind-eduard-bodaks-portfolio/)). Slop motion is the opposite: the same fade-up on every section, bounce or elastic easing everywhere, `animate-bounce`, `animate-pulse` and `transition-all` in the source ([Pixelslop](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/pixelslop@0.3.8/dist/skill/resources/ai-slop-patterns.md)), and count-up stats. Anthropic's alternative is "one well-orchestrated page load with staggered reveals" ([Anthropic Cookbook](https://platform.claude.com/cookbook/coding-prompting-for-frontend-aesthetics)).

For this studio, the budget is one choreographed hero sequence (a split-headline stagger, a mask reveal on the hero image, a nav that settles last), then mostly static content with motion tied to meaning. Examples are a route line drawn across the parish map, a before/after slider, and a satisfying state change on quote-form submit. Micro-interactions should follow the same rule. Animate link underlines with `scale-x` or `background-size` from the left at about 200–250 ms ease-out, paired with `:focus-visible`. Use at most one magnetic primary CTA, gated to fine pointers. Run a CSS-transform marquee for services or reviews that pauses on hover and focus, stops under reduced motion, and marks the duplicate as `aria-hidden`. Use a number ticker only for a real client-supplied figure, with the final value in the HTML. Use sticky stacking cards for a genuine process. These patterns are inferences from the principles above. No primary source specifically studies magnetic buttons or marquees.

Native CSS now covers much of this without JavaScript. **Scroll-driven animations became Baseline in all three engines on 27 October 2026** with Firefox 159 ([web-features](https://web-platform-dx.github.io/web-features-explorer/features/scroll-driven-animations), [web.dev](https://web.dev/blog/web-platform-10-2026)). Same-document View Transitions have been Baseline since October 2025 ([web-features](https://web-platform-dx.github.io/web-features-explorer/features/view-transitions)), and `@starting-style` since August 2024 ([web-features](https://web-platform-dx.github.io/web-features-explorer/features/starting-style/)). For multi-page static sites, cross-document view transitions (`@view-transition { navigation: auto; }`) work in Chrome 126 and Safari 18.2 but **not Firefox**. That is a free enhancement, with plain navigation as the fallback ([web-features](https://web-platform-dx.github.io/web-features-explorer/features/cross-document-view-transitions)). `interpolate-size` is Chromium-only ([web-features](https://web-platform-dx.github.io/web-features-explorer/features/interpolate-size)), and `text-wrap: pretty` is in Chrome and Safari 26 but not Firefox ([web-features](https://web-platform-dx.github.io/web-features-explorer/features/text-wrap-pretty/)). All of these should sit behind `@supports` and `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`, with a static default. Tailwind v3 has no utilities for them, so they belong in a small hand-written CSS layer.

For vendored JavaScript, the research measured gzip-9 sizes from the npm tarballs on 18 November 2026. These figures were not cross-checked against Bundlephobia.

GSAP became free for all commercial use, all former Club plugins included, with version 3.13 on 30 April 2025 ([Webflow](https://webflow.com/blog/gsap-becomes-free)). The licence explicitly permits use "on any website". It is **not open source**: Webflow keeps the IP, may amend the licence, and prohibits use in competing no-code animation builders. Rejecting an amendment still lets you use earlier versions, so the routine should **pin the vendored version and keep the licence headers** ([GSAP Standard License](https://gsap.com/community/standard-license/)). `gsap.matchMedia()` with a `reduceMotion` condition gives a clean reduced-motion switch and reverts automatically ([GSAP docs](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/GSAP/gsap.matchMedia()/)). Lenis integrates via `lenis.on('scroll', ScrollTrigger.update)` and the GSAP ticker. It keeps native scroll semantics but doesn't support CSS scroll-snap, and Safari caps it at 60fps ([Lenis](https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis)). The measured sizes undercut a common assumption. Without a bundler, the MIT alternatives are no lighter than GSAP core, so a default of GSAP core + ScrollTrigger + Lenis, with plugins per site, keeps a rich site **under about 70 KB gz** of motion code. Three.js (about 409 KB gz unminified as shipped) and Spline are poor fits. Spline also has no npm licence field and fetches scenes from Spline's servers, which conflicts with the vendoring rule ([npm three](https://registry.npmjs.org/three/latest), [npm @splinetool/runtime](https://registry.npmjs.org/@splinetool/runtime/latest)).

## Copy that only this business could say is the cheapest anti-slop upgrade

AI website copy is "weightless": "Build faster. Ship smarter." is correct but says nothing about who it is for ([925Studios](https://www.925studios.co/blog/ai-slop-design-tells)). The recurring markers are buzzwords such as "seamless" and "cutting-edge", where the fix is to replace each superlative with a number, a named capability or a before-and-after claim. Em-dash density and stock openers ("In today's fast-paced world") are the other big one; as Utsubo puts it, "The punctuation itself is fine. The density is the signal" ([Utsubo](https://www.utsubo.com/blog/built-with-ai-trust-signals-2026)). Wikipedia's editor guide lists further patterns that transfer directly to site copy. They include puffery that reads like "the transcript of a TV commercial", negative parallelisms ("not… but…"), forced rule-of-three triads, "-ing" tack-ons ("…, highlighting its commitment to…"), vague attributions, synonym-cycling, title-case headings, bolded inline headers, emoji bullets, and sudden shifts in English variety. The guide also cautions that these are observations, not proof, and that clusters are the real signal ([Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing, mirror](https://cavac.at/cavacopedia/Wikipedia%3ASigns_of_AI_writing)). Identical CTA labels used three or more times, and a subhead that repeats the heading with more than 40% word overlap, are mechanical tells that a script can catch ([Pixelslop](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/pixelslop@0.3.8/dist/skill/resources/ai-slop-patterns.md)).

The positive standard is simple: "Ask whether the founder would say it out loud" ([Kosta Canatselis](https://world.hey.com/kostac/spot-the-slop-a-ui-designer-s-guide-to-fixing-ai-defaults-4c448c9c)). Take a clear position about who the business is for ([Utsubo](https://www.utsubo.com/blog/built-with-ai-trust-signals-2026)). "Name what the button does, and delete any number you cannot source" ([Capital & Compute](https://capitalandcompute.net/blog/fix-ai-slop-design/)). For trades, add an About page that says something real about who the owner is and how long they have worked, state the service area in body copy, and say plainly if quotes are free ([b:web](https://bwebsites.co.uk/news/what-makes-a-good-website-for-a-trades-business/)). Here is an illustrative rewrite of the default trade hero. Instead of "Professional Window Cleaning Services You Can Trust" with "Get Started", write "Windows, gutters and conservatory roofs cleaned across St Peter Port, St Martin and the Vale, every four or eight weeks", with a "Call Darren" button showing the number. That version names parishes, services, frequency and a person. British spelling and local references (parishes, "town", "the Bridge") cost nothing and also avoid the English-variety-shift tell. A copy lint should ban the usual words (seamless, elevate, unlock, empower, cutting-edge, delve, "look no further", "we pride ourselves"). It should flag more than about one em dash per 150 words, "not just X, but Y" constructions, three-adjective triads, repeated CTA labels, American spellings and any number not present in client data. It should also require at least one place name or concrete service detail per section. The thresholds are this report's proposal, built from the cited patterns.

## The routine needs friction at intake and a deterministic gate at the end

The research converges on a five-stage pipeline, and each stage maps onto a concrete tool. **Intake** is the "productive friction" stage. Collect the logo or vector, livery and flyer colours, real job photos, owner name, parishes served, the services and prices, accreditations, verbatim reviews and the phone number. Refuse to invent stats or testimonials. **Specification** produces a per-client DESIGN.md built on CodeMySpec's five decisions: theme, colour values per role, type (1–2 families and a scale), layout (nav style, content width, page shapes), and tone (density, radius, shadow depth, one feeling word). Their example sets radius at 0.5rem on fields and 1rem on cards, with hairline borders and one depth step ([CodeMySpec](https://codemyspec.com/blog/vibe-coded-websites-look-the-same)). Add the chosen hero archetype and the trade motif. The spec values are written straight into `tailwind.config.js` so no default token survives. **Generation** runs under an Anthropic-style aesthetics block. Its key lines are "Commit to a cohesive aesthetic", "Vary between light and dark themes, different fonts, different aesthetics" and an explicit avoid list ([Anthropic Cookbook](https://platform.claude.com/cookbook/coding-prompting-for-frontend-aesthetics)). The block is extended with the second-wave bans and the merged font ban, and it states that palette and type come from the spec, not from the model. Asking for three distinct directions and rejecting the first, most generic one is a cheap extra lever ([prg.sh](https://prg.sh/ramblings/Why-Your-AI-Keeps-Building-the-Same-Purple-Gradient-Website)). Instructions alone are unreliable, though: "deterministic scanners are more reliable than instructions the agent may ignore" ([Capital & Compute](https://capitalandcompute.net/blog/fix-ai-slop-design/)).

**Linting** is where the detector catalogues pay off. Impeccable covers 37 patterns, 25 of them deterministic via `npx impeccable detect` ([Impeccable](https://git.secluded.site/agent-skills/blob/main/vendor/impeccable/site/pages/slop/index.astro)). Pixelslop's core test is "Did a human make this choice, or did the AI fall back to a default?", and it supplies thresholds and grep signals ([Pixelslop](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/pixelslop@0.3.8/dist/skill/resources/ai-slop-patterns.md)). Krebs showed the same checks run cheaply in Playwright against computed styles ([Krebs](https://adriankrebs.ch/blog/design-slop/)). A post-build Playwright lint for this routine should fail the build on the conditions below.

| Check | Threshold (from Pixelslop/Impeccable unless noted) |
|---|---|
| Centred text | >70% of headings and paragraphs centred |
| Glass | 3+ elements with `backdrop-filter` |
| Gradient text | Any `background-clip: text` on headings or metrics |
| Card uniformity | 5+ containers with 8–20 px radius plus shadow; 3+ sibling cards with identical DOM |
| Section repetition | 3+ consecutive sections with the same structure |
| CTAs | ≥3 identical CTA labels |
| Colour | Pure #000/#fff body ground; Tailwind indigo/violet/purple utilities (`from-purple`, `via-blue`) |
| Motion | Bounce/overshoot cubic-beziers; `animate-bounce`, `animate-pulse`, `transition-all` |
| Fonts | Any family from the merged ban list |
| Contrast | Body below WCAG AA 4.5:1 |
| Copy | Ban-word hits, em-dash density, repeated heading/subhead words >40% |

Other named tools (avoid-ai-design, which exits with code 2 for CI; Gesso with 73 rules; no-slop-ui; antislop) are reported second-hand and were not verified ([Capital & Compute](https://capitalandcompute.net/blog/fix-ai-slop-design/)). Run the **human pass** last. Look at the site, use it on a bad connection and a small screen, and watch someone else use it without correcting them ([Kosta Canatselis](https://world.hey.com/kostac/spot-the-slop-a-ui-designer-s-guide-to-fixing-ai-defaults-4c448c9c)). Finish with a specific meta description, a custom OG image generated from the palette, and descriptive alt text ([Utsubo](https://www.utsubo.com/blog/built-with-ai-trust-signals-2026)).

Figma helps most when it holds the human decisions, not when it generates layouts. NN/g found Figma First Draft output generic, with weak hierarchy and only minor variation across attempts, which "seriously undermine[s] the tool's value for ideation". Narrow features such as Rename Layers were the useful ones ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-design-tools-update-2/)). A critique of Figma Sites reports that background patterns and gradients are dropped on publish, ARIA labels don't carry over, nested URLs are flattened, and Make inside Sites invents its own text and layout ([UX Planet](https://uxplanet.org/why-figma-sites-beta-isnt-closing-the-gap-yet-058ce342d71e)). The productive route is the **Figma MCP server**. Design one or two key frames with the client's real photos and livery colours. Then have the agent call `get_variable_defs` for tokens, `get_screenshot` for visual reference and `get_motion_context` for prototyped motion. Call `get_design_context` with an explicit "plain HTML and Tailwind v3 classes, no React" instruction, because its default output "looks like React and Tailwind" and is meant as context rather than finished code. On the remote server, pass a frame link ([Figma MCP docs](https://developers.figma.com/docs/figma-mcp-server/server-returning-web-code), [Tools and prompts](https://developers.figma.com/docs/figma-mcp-server/tools-and-prompts/)). Among plugins, Tokens Studio or Design Tokens W3C Export feed the Tailwind config, and Iconify and Phosphor cover icons. Stark, axe for Designers and Contrast cover accessibility checks. Rename It and Cleaner handle housekeeping ([Muzli](https://muz.li/blog/best-figma-plugins-for-designers-in-2026/)). UX Pilot, Relume and Uizard are generators and should be kept out of final layouts.

## Conclusion

This research changes the problem from "make it look less AI" to "never let the model make an unanchored choice". Every slop pattern is a default appearing "regardless of subject" ([CodeMySpec](https://codemyspec.com/blog/vibe-coded-websites-look-the-same)), and every escape route a prompt names, from Fraunces to cream-and-terracotta, becomes the next default within a year. The only durable defence is to source decisions from outside the model, and trade clients make that unusually easy. They already have a van, a flyer, a phone number, a set of parishes, a face and a portfolio of real jobs. A Guernsey studio's competitive edge is therefore its intake. A routine that extracts those assets, writes them into the Tailwind theme and refuses to fill gaps with invented content will produce sites that look hand-made, because the inputs are hand-made.

Two implications follow for running the routine over time. First, the ban lists are perishable. The studio should rerun its own Playwright lint across its portfolio periodically, and add any font, palette or layout that starts recurring across its own clients, since the studio's house style is as much a risk as the model's. Second, the evidence base has gaps. No study measures slop prevalence or conversion effects on small local-business sites, and most hero exemplars here are unverified leads. A few hours spent saving verified screenshots of genuinely good trade sites into a private reference library would give the routine better reference material than the SaaS-heavy galleries the model already imitates.
